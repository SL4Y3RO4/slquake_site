About SLQUAKE

SLQUAKE is a competitive multiplayer mod for Quake 1, that aim to make quake more competitive and fun to play, with new mechanics, weapons, maps and more.
This mod take example to others arena fps games, titanfall 2 and apex legends for create a fast gameplay adding more verticality, where a player must try our best to having better against his opponents and win game.
This project I've been started from 2025 to make Quake 1 better for competitive side. A GAME THAT DONT GIVE YOU A REAL CHALLENGE, IS A LOSER GAME
