About DUELIST

DUELIST is a competitive multiplayer mod for Quake 1, that aim to make quake more competitive and fun to play, with new mechanics, weapons, maps and more.
This mod take example to others arena fps games, like titanfall 2 and doom eternal for create a fast gameplay adding more, then player will must fight against a very vertical gameplay to try his best to having better against his opponents and win game.
This project I've been started from 2025 to make Quake 1 better for competitive side in my way. A GAME THAT DONT GIVE YOU A REAL CHALLENGE, IS A LOSER GAME
